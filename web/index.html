A form coach that lives in your browser tab.

Prop up your laptop, start a set, and Kinetiq counts every rep. It tells you the moment your squat gets shallow or your hips start to sag.

Try it with your camera See how it works

From camera to coaching in three steps

Every step runs on your own device. Nothing is installed, and nothing is uploaded.

  1. Your camera sees you

    Open the page and allow camera access. Any laptop webcam or phone camera works. Stand where your whole body is in frame.

  2. Pose tracking finds your joints

    A lightweight pose model marks your shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles on every frame.

  3. The motion model reads your movement

    Kinetiq's transformer watches the last two seconds of motion. It names the exercise, tracks where you are in each rep, and spots form faults.

What it coaches today

Kinetiq recognises the exercise on its own. Just start moving.

ExerciseWhat it watches for
SquatsDepth that stops short of parallel, and a chest that folds forward.
Push-upsHalf reps, hips sagging toward the floor, and hips piking up.
LungesA back knee that never gets low, and leaning over the front leg.
Jumping jacksArms that stop at shoulder height instead of going overhead.

Your video never leaves your device

Both models run inside the browser with WebAssembly. Kinetiq has no server that receives frames, so there is nothing to store, leak or sell. You can switch off your Wi-Fi after the page loads and it keeps counting.
